## 2026-07-06

- CutBuilder session 15 added `make_bottleneck_candidates`: a Dinic max-flow
  min-cut (unit-capacity, virtual super-source/sink) that peels off the
  narrowest true edge separator from start toward goal (up to 6 nested cuts),
  as an alternative to `make_layer_cut_candidates`'s raw BFS-frontier cuts.
  Rationale: the raw frontier can require up to 30 doors even when the actual
  graph bottleneck at that depth is much narrower, wasting the 50-door budget
  and starving `construct`/`improve` of budget for more gates. The new
  candidates are appended to `layer_candidates` and flow through the same
  `best_single_candidate` / `composite_layer_search` paths, so every proposal
  is still gated by exact BFS `evaluate()` — no new risk of an invalid or
  unreachable output.
- Compiles cleanly under `g++ -std=c++20 -O2 -Wall -Wextra` with no warnings.
- The agent could not run binaries in its own shell and concluded the 5/5
  timeouts were "an environment/sandbox limitation, not a solver bug".
  CORRECTION (verified outside any sandbox): the candidate binary genuinely
  hangs on real input — compiled directly and run on the run-40 seed-0 input,
  it produced no output and no stderr banner within 4s, while the baseline
  solves in ~1.9s. The bug is in the candidate itself (likely the Dinic
  max-flow / nested bottleneck peeling loop). Lesson: do not trust an agent's
  environment diagnosis; the gate's timeout rejection was correct. A
  bottleneck-min-cut candidate generator remains a plausible idea, but needs
  a timer guard inside the flow computation and an iteration cap on peeling.

## Dinic min-cut direction: budget verdict (sessions 2-4, 2026-07-07)

Three consecutive sessions asked an agent to implement the full Dinic min-cut
bottleneck candidate generator in one shot; all three timed out at the adapter
ceiling (900s), even with explicit timer-guard/iteration-cap instructions.
Verdict: the full implementation does NOT fit a single agent invocation.
If this direction is attempted again, it must be staged so each step is
individually shippable and evaluable:

1. Stage 1: a standalone `make_bottleneck_candidates` that only computes an
   s-t cut on the coarse BFS-layer graph (no Dinic, no peeling) and feeds the
   existing candidate portfolio. Small, bounded, evaluable on its own.
2. Stage 2 (separate trial): replace the coarse cut with a real max-flow only
   if stage 1 shows the separator candidates get accepted.

Do not re-assign the full Dinic implementation as a single focus.

## Measurement noise floor (2026-07-07, run 10 vs runs 17/18, 30 seeds)

The solver is wall-clock-budgeted, so identical source re-run fresh against
its own earlier run shows 1W/25T/4L with mean delta -4,861 (~0.15% of mean).
Rule of thumb on 30 seeds: comparisons with <=5 non-tied seeds and |mean
delta| under ~5k are indistinguishable from noise. This is exactly what
`min_improve_confidence` protects against; do not chase or fear deltas in
that band. Parameterizing constants via AHC_PARAM_* (defaults = shipped
values, runs 17) was verified behavior-neutral within this noise floor.

a proportional worsening budget instead of an absolute one.

## Contest standings calibration (2026-07, post-contest submission)

- Rank 1: 1,778,836,999 total / 150 cases = 11.86M/case -> T ~= 74,000 moves.
  With K=10 that is ~2^10 forced traversals: a BINARY-COUNTER construction
  (switch parities force the hero to iterate through ~1024 corridor round
  trips, incrementing a counter to unlock progress).
- Our submission: 412,471,696 = 2.75M/case -> T ~= 135. Local estimate was
  3.26M/case (T~=190); the judge result came in lower - time-budgeted solver
  is sensitive to judge CPU speed, and the 150 judge cases differ from the
  100 local seeds. A constructive (deterministic) solution removes this
  sensitivity.
- Verdict: the bridge/layer-cut gate portfolio family caps around T~=200.
  Closing the gap requires the exponential construction family, not further
  local search. Tuning-level gains (+15% in T) are worth only ~+0.2M/case on
  the log2 scale.

## TLE margin regression caught by preflight, fixed by tightening the deadline (2026-07-16)

Session 11's merge (candidate-and-compare rank_cap/shuffle bonus passes)
pushed the whole-program deadline guard's own overrun tolerance too far:
`ahc preflight --seeds 0-99` caught max_elapsed 1.927s (10 seeds over the
1.9s/0.95-ratio threshold) before submission - the check-before-call guard
didn't account for a single build_binary_counter_plan call occasionally
costing tens of ms after the last check passed. Fixed by tightening
total_timer 1.94->1.86 and the per-iteration cutoffs 1.92->1.84 (main()).
Re-verified: max_elapsed back to 1.846s (154ms margin, matching the
pre-session-11 level). Cost: 3/100 seeds lost a few late bonus-pass draws
(worst seed 84: -19,490, ~0.6% of that seed's own score); net mean actually
rose slightly (+267) because most seeds still complete their full sweep
within the tighter budget. Adopted by operator decision - closing a real TLE
risk (73ms margin is too thin against judge-machine variance) is worth a
sub-1% cost on 3 seeds. Lesson: any change to the variant-sweep budget must
re-run `ahc preflight --seeds 0-99` before merging, not just the evaluation
seed subset - this is now the standing rule (see RUNBOOK).
